# Sources for the US/UK starting-solids examples

The scripts are short creative examples, not personalised feeding advice. Their factual points are based on:

- CDC, [When, What, and How to Introduce Solid Foods](https://www.cdc.gov/infant-toddler-nutrition/foods-and-drinks/when-what-and-how-to-introduce-solid-foods.html): around six months, readiness signs, mashed textures, and supervision.
- NHS, [Your baby's first solid foods](https://www.nhs.uk/baby/weaning-and-feeding/babys-first-solid-foods/): UK use of “weaning”, around six months, and progression of textures.
- NHS, [Preparing food safely for babies](https://www.nhs.uk/best-start-in-life/baby/weaning/safe-weaning/preparing-food-safely/): upright seating and supervision.
